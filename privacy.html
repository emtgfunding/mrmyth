Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Mr. Myth website (the "Site") handles information about visitors. The Site is a simple promotional page for the book Mr. Myth — Animals of the World. We try to collect as little as possible.

1. Who we are

The Site is operated by Mr. Myth Collection ("we", "us", "our"). For any privacy question, you can reach us at mustafajaber312@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

The Site does not have signup forms, contact forms, newsletter forms, comments, login, or shopping cart. We do not run analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tracking cookies. We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it directly.

3. Information automatically processed

Like any website, our hosting provider may automatically receive standard request data when you load a page, including:

  • Your IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • The page requested and the time of the request
  • The referring URL

This data is used by the hosting provider to deliver the page and protect the Site from abuse. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

4. Third-party services

To make the Site work, your browser loads resources from a small number of third parties. When it does, those parties may receive your IP address and basic request data subject to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Vercel — the Site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's privacy policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Amazon — when you click a "Buy" link, you are taken to Amazon.com, which operates under its own privacy policy: amazon.com privacy notice.

5. Cookies

The Site itself does not set cookies. Third-party resources listed above generally do not set cookies in this configuration, but their behavior is controlled by them and may change. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time.

6. Children's privacy

The book Mr. Myth — Animals of the World is written for children ages 2 and up, but the Site is intended for parents and adult buyers. The Site does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we do not run forms, signups, or accounts, there is nothing for a child to submit. If you are a parent or guardian and have any concern, please contact us at mustafajaber312@gmail.com.

7. Your rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA)

Even though we do not directly collect personal information through the Site, applicable laws may give you rights with respect to data processed by our hosting provider or by the third parties listed above. These typically include the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights with respect to data we control, email mustafajaber312@gmail.com. For data controlled by Google, Vercel, or Amazon, please contact them directly using the links above.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as defined under California law.

8. Data retention

We do not maintain a database of visitor information. Server logs maintained by our hosting provider are retained according to their standard policy.

9. Security

The Site is served over HTTPS. We take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we handle, but no transmission over the internet can be guaranteed 100% secure.

10. Changes to this policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email mustafajaber312@gmail.com.
