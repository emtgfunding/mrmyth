Six little friends.
One big adventure.

Each story takes Mr. Myth — and your child — to a new corner of the world.

Story 01 The Savanna
— Leo the Lion —

The Brave Lion of the Golden Plains

Deep in the warm golden plains, a lion named Leo watched the tall grass sway in the wind.

Leo had a mighty roar… but today, he felt unsure. “What if I'm not brave enough?” he wondered quietly.

The wind whispered, the sun glowed… and Leo took a deep breath. Then suddenly —

ROOOAR!

Story 02 The River
— Ella the Elephant —

The Gentle Giant of the River

Near a calm river, an elephant named Ella played in the water. Ella was strong… but sometimes, she felt too big.

“What if I don't fit in?” she wondered.

SPLASH!

She lifted her trunk high and played in the water. In that moment, Ella smiled. Being different made her special.

Story 03 The Ice
— Pip the Penguin —

The Little Penguin on the Ice

On a cold sheet of ice, a penguin named Pip waddled carefully. Pip loved to swim… but the water looked so deep.

“What if I fall?” he wondered. Pip took a tiny step… then another.

SLIP!

He slid right into the water — and started to swim! Pip spun and splashed happily. “I can do this!” he chirped.

Story 04 The Bamboo Forest
— Bao the Panda —

The Playful Panda

In a quiet bamboo forest where the sun trickled through the leaves, a little panda rolled in the soft green grass.

He tumbled and laughed and chewed on tender shoots, while Mr. Myth lay close by — watching, smiling, listening.

Some adventures are loud. Others are gentle. Both are magic.

Story 05 The Open Savanna
— Gia the Giraffe —

The Tall Giraffe in the Savanna

On the wide open savanna, a giraffe named Gia looked around. Gia was very tall… taller than everyone else.

“Why am I so different?” she wondered. Gia bent down low… then stretched up high.

REACH!

She nibbled leaves from the tallest tree. Gia smiled proudly. Being tall made her special.

Story 06 The Jungle
— Taro the Tiger —

The Brave Tiger in the Jungle

In a deep green jungle, a tiger named Taro walked quietly. Taro was strong… but sometimes he felt unsure.

“What if I'm not brave enough?” he wondered. Taro took a slow step… then another.

ROAR!

His voice echoed through the trees. Taro stood tall and proud.
